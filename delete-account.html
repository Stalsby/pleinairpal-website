Delete your Plein Air Pal account

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and data for the Plein Air Pal app on iPhone and Android. You can ask in the app, or by email if you can no longer open the app.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Plein Air Pal and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab, tap the three-dot (...) button at the top right, then choose Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the message and tap Continue.
  5. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete Account.

You are signed out right away.

Delete your account by email

If you cannot use the app, email info@pleinairpal.com with the subject "Delete my Plein Air Pal account". Send it from the email address on your account. We may ask you to confirm the request, so we know it comes from you. We aim to reply within 30 days.

What happens after you ask

  • Right away. Your account is deactivated and hidden from other users in the app.
  • For the next 30 days. This is a grace period. If you change your mind, sign in to Plein Air Pal during these 30 days and choose to reactivate your account. Everything comes back as it was.
  • After the 30 days. Your account and its data are permanently deleted by the next daily clean-up. This cannot be undone. If the clean-up cannot finish everything in one run, it continues at the next one.

What is deleted

When the clean-up runs, it removes your files first and then your account:

  • Your account, including your sign-in details and email address.
  • Your profile, including your display name, bio and website.
  • Your profile photo.
  • Your paintings and their photos, with their details and map locations.
  • Your events and their cover images.
  • Your comments, likes and follows, and the other activity linked to your account.
  • Your shared live location, if you had it on.

What may be kept

  • Limited records that we are required to keep by law.
  • Subscription and payment records, which are kept by Apple, Google and our subscription provider RevenueCat under their own policies.
  • Email delivery records, such as records of the verification and password reset emails we sent you, which may be kept by our email provider for a limited period.
  • Crash reports and usage analytics, which are kept by our service providers for a limited period under their own retention settings and are then deleted.
  • Copies in our providers' backups, which may remain for a short time before they are overwritten.

For more detail, see the Privacy Policy.

Your subscription is not cancelled

Deleting your account does not cancel a Plein Air Pal Pro subscription. To stop being charged, cancel it yourself in your Apple ID subscription settings (App Store) or in Google Play, before or after you delete your account.

Questions

If you have a question about deleting your account, contact us at info@pleinairpal.com.
