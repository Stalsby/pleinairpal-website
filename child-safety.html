Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Our commitment

Plein Air Pal is a community app for outdoor painters. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, and any content that sexualizes minors. This page explains our standards, how to report a concern, and how we respond.

Who can use the app

Plein Air Pal is intended for adults only. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account and use the app, as set out in our Terms of Use.

What is not allowed

We do not allow any content or conduct that depicts, promotes, solicits or facilitates the sexual abuse or exploitation of minors. This applies everywhere in the app, including:

  • Photos, paintings, images and videos
  • Comments and messages
  • Profile names, bios and avatars
  • Event listings and painting locations

Accounts that break this rule are permanently removed.

How to report a concern

You can report content and accounts inside the app, and you can block users at any time. You can also email us directly at info@pleinairpal.com. Reports about child safety are treated as the highest priority.

How we respond

When we receive a report or find a violation, we:

  • Review it promptly and remove the content.
  • Suspend or permanently ban the accounts involved.
  • Preserve relevant information as required by law.

Reporting to authorities

We report apparent CSAM and CSAE to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) through its CyberTipline, and to the relevant national and regional authorities. We cooperate with valid law enforcement requests, and we comply with applicable child safety laws.

Contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety and CSAM prevention matters is info@pleinairpal.com.
