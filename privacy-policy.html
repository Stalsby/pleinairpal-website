Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Introduction

Plein Air Pal is a mobile app for plein air painters, available on iPhone and Android. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the developer of Plein Air Pal.

If you have a question about your privacy, contact us at info@pleinairpal.com.

Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your email address, your password and your display name. Your password is stored by our authentication provider in a protected form, and we cannot read it. You can also add a profile photo, a bio and a website.

Content you create

  • Paintings and photos you upload, and the details you add to them, such as title, medium, size, description, date and location.
  • Comments you post.
  • Event listings you post, including the details you enter, such as the organizer, description, venue, address, price, contact details and registration link.
  • The people you follow, the paintings you like, and any users you block or content you report.

Location

  • Your position on the map. With your permission, the app uses your device's location to show where you are and what is nearby. Your device lets you choose precise or approximate location.
  • Locations you choose to attach. When you pin a painting or post an event, the location you set is saved with it.
  • Live location sharing, which is optional and off unless you turn it on. See the Location section below for how it works.

Subscription information

If you subscribe to Plein Air Pal Pro, the purchase is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. We receive your subscription status, such as whether it is active and when it renews, so we can give you access to Pro features.

Usage and device information

We collect information about how the app is used, such as screens viewed, features used and session length, together with basic device information such as device type, operating system and app version. We use it to understand which features work and to improve the app.

Crash and diagnostic information

If the app crashes or hits an error, a crash report is sent so we can fix the problem. It can include the error details, your device type, operating system and app version.

Information sent when you connect

Like most online services, the providers we use receive your device's IP address and basic connection details when the app connects to them.

How we use your information

  • To run the app: your account, your profile, the map, paintings, comments, events and the people you follow.
  • To show your public content to other users, as described below.
  • To provide Pro features to subscribers.
  • To send account emails, such as sign-up verification codes and password reset codes.
  • To keep the community safe, including reviewing reports, enforcing our Community Guidelines and preventing abuse.
  • To find and fix crashes and errors.
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show ads in the app, and we do not use your information for advertising.

What other users can see

Plein Air Pal is a community app, so some of what you add is visible to other users:

  • Your profile: display name, profile photo, bio and website.
  • Paintings you post, with their details and their location on the map.
  • Your comments.
  • Events you post, including the contact details you enter for the event, and your name as the person who posted it.
  • Your live location, only while live location sharing is on.

Think about what you post before you share it, especially contact details and exact locations.

Location

You can allow or deny location access in your device settings at any time. If you deny it, some features, such as showing your position on the map, will not work.

Live location sharing lets other signed-in users see your current position on the map. It is off unless you turn it on. While it is on and the app is open on the map, your position is updated about every 15 seconds. When you close the app or switch sharing off, updates stop. Other users stop seeing your position about 5 minutes after the last update. Switching sharing off deletes your shared position right away, and any old position left behind is deleted automatically within a few hours.

Service providers we use

We use trusted service providers to run the app. They process information on our behalf, only to provide their service to us, under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Supabase for the database, file storage and account sign-in.
  • RevenueCat for managing subscriptions and access to Pro features.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play for app distribution and payments.
  • Mapbox for maps. When you view the map, your device requests map data from Mapbox, which receives the area being viewed.
  • Resend for sending account emails, such as verification and password reset codes.
  • Sentry for crash and error reports.
  • PostHog for usage analytics.
  • Expo for delivering app updates.

Some of these providers store or process information in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, we rely on the safeguards these providers offer for international transfers.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect the safety of our users or others, or as part of a sale or transfer of the app, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

How long we keep information

  • We keep your account information and content for as long as your account exists. If you delete a painting, comment or event, it is removed from the app.
  • When you delete your account, it is marked for deletion right away, and your account and its associated data are permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Live location positions are kept only as described in the Location section.
  • Crash reports and usage analytics are kept by our providers for a limited period under their retention settings, and are then deleted.
  • Copies in our providers' backups may remain for a short time before they are overwritten.
  • Subscription and payment records are kept by Apple and Google under their own policies, and we may keep limited records where the law requires it.

Delete your Plein Air Pal account

You can delete your Plein Air Pal account and its associated data in either of these ways:

In the app

Open Settings, choose to delete your account, and confirm by typing DELETE.

By email

Email info@pleinairpal.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Plein Air Pal account". We may ask you to confirm the request, so we know it comes from you.

What happens next

Your account is marked for deletion right away, and your account, profile, paintings, comments, events, likes, follows and shared location are permanently deleted within 30 days. Limited records may be kept where the law requires it, as described above.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription. To stop being charged, cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings or in Google Play.

Your rights and choices

You can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete. You can also edit your profile in the app.
  • Delete your account and information, as described above.
  • Restrict or stop certain uses of your information.

You can turn off location access and notifications in your device settings, and turn off live location sharing in the app, at any time.

To make a request, email info@pleinairpal.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we use your information to provide the service you signed up for, with your consent for location and notifications, for our legitimate interests in keeping the app safe, reliable and improving, and to meet legal obligations. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Information is encrypted when it travels between the app and our providers, and our database provider encrypts stored data. Access to data in our database is limited by access rules, so users can only change their own information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Age requirement

Plein Air Pal is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we will let you know in the app or by email.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or your information, contact us at info@pleinairpal.com.
